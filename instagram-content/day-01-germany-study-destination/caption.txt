India is now the #1 source of international students in Germany. 🇮🇳🇩🇪

In Winter Semester 2024/25, nearly 59,000 Indian students were enrolled — a big jump from previous years.

Why are so many Indian students choosing Germany?
• Public universities with very low or zero tuition
• Strong focus on Engineering, IT, Management & Applied Sciences
• English-taught Master's programmes available
• Post-study work options and pathway to long-term stay
• Globally respected degrees

Germany is no longer a "backup" option. For many Indian students, it has become the first choice.
